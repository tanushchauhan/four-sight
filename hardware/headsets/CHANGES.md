# Headset CAD changes

- Integrated the phone corner locators into the iPhone 16 Pro door for rigid printing, replacing separate flexible inserts.
- Enlarged the shell and matching door for the iPhone 16 Pro Max while retaining the lens carriage and focus wheel geometry.
- Revised the Pro Max camera opening and restored the continuous camera-side corner wall, retaining a small underside clearance relief.
- Added strap adjusters for 1-inch and 1.5-inch elastic, plus one STL containing three of each size.

Each headset uses its model-specific shell and door, plus the shared lens carriage and focus wheel. The combined strap STL is an alternative print layout to the individual adjuster files.

These are the latest selected prototypes. Digital mesh checks do not establish physical fit or strength; phone fit and strap retention require testing.
