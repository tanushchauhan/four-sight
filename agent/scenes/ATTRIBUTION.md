# Photo attribution

Real photographs used by the photo scenes in `manifest-photos.json`. Each was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (the 1280 px rendition of the original file), then cropped and/or resized to at most 1024 px, stripped of metadata and re-encoded as JPEG under 170 KB. Public-domain U.S. government works carry no copyright; CC-licensed files are used under the stated license. The training-exercise photos show role-players, moulage (fake wounds) and mannequins, not real casualties.
