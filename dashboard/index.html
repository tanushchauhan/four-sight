Manual control & robot actions

Released

Take control on this Mac to stop autonomous exploration and pause the glove relay. In autonomous mode, click the centered USB stick once to take over, then again to enable. E enables, hold WASD to move, space stops. Release returns control to the glove. With WASD released, your headset turns the robot. Direction keys override head steering; space disables both.

AirTag test: place Prada on level ground with the rear clear. Tilts back to nominal 17°, rocks three times between 8° and 17°, and returns level. Stop cancels after the current pose transition; it is not an instant power cutoff.
